# Accommodation Shortlist — 7 Overnight Stops

**Trip:** 29 June – 10 July 2026 · couple + 1-year-old · own car (UK plates)
**Criteria:** kitchen/kitchenette, parking, cot (or space for travel cot), walkable location, ~€100–180/night
**Excluded:** Basel (sorted) and Maastricht (relatives)

⭐ = my top pick for that stop. All properties verified as currently operating; prices are indicative — check live rates for your dates.

---

## 1. Reims — Mon 29 June

### ⭐ Residhome Appart Hotel Reims Centre
Aparthotel, 6 rue de Courcelles — 200 m from station, 10–12 min walk to cathedral. Kitchenette in every studio; on-site parking €10/night; **free baby cot**. ~€89–120/night. 8.0/10 Booking (1,863 reviews).
**Why:** ticks every requirement in one go — and after the 2 h 40 from Calais you can walk straight to dinner.
[Residhome](https://www.residhome.com/uk/hotel-residence-aparthotel-reims-233.html) · [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/fr/residhome-reims-centre.en-gb.html)

### Appart'City Confort Reims Centre
Aparthotel, 700 m from cathedral. Full kitchenette; ~€63–110/night. 8.1/10 Booking.
**Why:** most walkable + cheapest — but **no on-site parking** (street/public nearby), the one caveat with a loaded car.
[Appart'City](https://www.appartcity.com/en/destinations/france/grand-est/reims/reims-centre) · [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/fr/appart-city-reims-centre.html)

### Séjours & Affaires Reims Clairmarais
Aparthotel near station, ~15 min walk to cathedral. Kitchenette; on-site parking €9/day; cot on request. ~€70–100/night. 7.9/10 Booking.
**Why:** cheapest secure-parking fallback; functional rather than charming.
[Séjours & Affaires](https://www.sejours-affaires.com/uk/hotel-residence-aparthotel-reims-177.html)

## 2. Troyes — Tue 30 June

### ⭐ SmartAppart Troyes
Aparthotel in the old town, 10 min walk from station. Equipped kitchenette; **cots offered**; paid public car park directly across the street. ~€60–90/night. **8.6/10 Booking** — best-reviewed in Troyes.
**Why:** right in the half-timbered centre at a great price; only compromise is the garage being across the road, not on-site.
[SmartAppart](https://www.smart-appart.fr/fr/hotels/troyes-smartappart-troyes/5) · [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/fr/smartappart-troyes.html)

### The Originals / Kosy Appart'hotels Troyes City & Park
Aparthotel ~800 m from cathedral. Kitchenette; **on-site parking ~€7/night**; cots subject to availability. ~€70–110/night. 7.7/10 Booking. No A/C — worth noting for a July heatwave.
**Why:** the choice if you want the car on the property itself.
[Kosy](https://en.kosy-apparthotels.com/troyes-city-park/) · [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/fr/apparthotel-city-amp-park.en-gb.html)

### Appart'Hôtel Sainte Trinité
7 apartments in a converted medieval building, heart of the old town. Full kitchenette + dishwasher, A/C; secure on-site parking ~€20/day (**tight spaces — reserve with the room**). ~€90–140/night. Mixed reviews; narrow medieval stairs — weigh that with baby + luggage.
**Why:** the character pick if atmosphere beats convenience.
[Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/fr/appart-39-sainte-trinite.html)

## 3. Beaune — Wed 1 July

### ⭐ Résidence Beaune Sweet Home
4 air-conditioned serviced apartments, 200–300 m from the Hospices. **Full kitchens**; **secure covered parking included**; **cot + highchair on request**. ~€130–180/night. **9.2/10 Booking (115 reviews)**.
**Why:** the strongest all-round match of the whole trip — explicitly baby-equipped, dead central, parking included.
[beaune-sweethome.fr](https://www.beaune-sweethome.fr/en/the-apartments/) · [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/fr/beaune-allerey.html)

### La Maison de Maurice
Designer apartments above a wine bar, 100 m from Notre-Dame. Full kitchen; **cots can be added**; no on-site parking (public garage ~300 m). ~€140–155/night. 9.1/10 Booking (463 reviews).
**Why:** superb reviews and the most central address possible; you trade parking for a 300 m walk.
[lamaisondemaurice-beaune.com](https://www.lamaisondemaurice-beaune.com/la-maison-de-maurice-beaune-en.html) · [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/fr/la-maison-de-maurice.html)

### Chez Marie – Centre – Parking privé
Host-run 1-bedroom apartment with terrace, central. Full kitchen; **free private on-site parking**; separate bedroom = easy travel-cot setup (message host re: cot). ~€110–140/night. Small review base.
**Why:** only central option with free on-site parking and a proper separate bedroom for baby's sleep.
[Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/fr/chez-coco-t2-proche-centre-avec-parking-47-terrasse.fr.html)

## 4. Titisee / Black Forest — Sun 5 July

> ⚠️ **Peak season + small properties — book these first, ideally this month.**

### ⭐ Ferienwohnungen DaHeim Titisee (Hinterzarten)
Family-run holiday apartments on the Hinterzarten side of the lake. Full kitchen incl. oven + dishwasher; **free parking**; **cot €6/night**. ~€94–130/night. 9.2/10 Booking.
**Why:** proper kitchen, free parking, cheap cot, top score, real Black Forest character.
[Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/de/ferienwohnungen-daheim-titisee.html) · [daheim.ferienhaeusle.com](https://daheim.ferienhaeusle.com/)

### Appartements Waldrose
9 apartments **directly on the lakefront** (Bruderhalde, away from the touristy promenade), own boat dock. Kitchenette; free parking; cot €10/stay. ~€110–160/night in July. 8.9/10.
**Why:** best lake position — morning pram walk along the water from your door.
[Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/de/appartements-waldrose.html)

### Ferienhaus Frei Apartments
Family-run apartments in Titisee-Neustadt, short drive to the lake. Full kitchens; free parking; **cot + highchair free**. ~€80–130/night — best value. Book direct.
**Why:** the value fallback if the lakeside two are sold out.
[ferienhaus-frei.com](https://ferienhaus-frei.com/)

## 5. Strasbourg — Mon 6 July

> ⚠️ **Crit'Air sticker required** for the low-emission zone (ANPR-enforced, €68 fine). Order now from [certificat-air.gouv.fr](https://www.certificat-air.gouv.fr/) — 2–4 weeks delivery to the UK. All three picks have on-site parking so the car sits untouched.

### ⭐ Aparthotel Adagio Access Strasbourg Petite France
117-unit aparthotel, 5 min walk to Petite France. Kitchenette in every unit; on-site parking ~€15/night (reserve); cot on request. ~€100–150/night. 8.1/10 Booking (3,500+ reviews).
**Why:** best location-plus-parking combination — park inside the zone and stroll into Petite France with the pram.
[Adagio](https://www.adagio-city.com/gb/hotel-8446-aparthotel-adagio-access-strasbourg-petite-france/index.shtml) · [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/fr/adagio-access-strasbourg-petite-france.html)

### Citadines Kléber Strasbourg
On Place Kléber — dead centre, 8–10 min to both Petite France and cathedral. Best kitchenette of the three (stovetop + dishwasher); secure underground parking ~€12–20/day; cot on request. ~€120–180/night. 8.4/10.
**Why:** the most central, with the best-equipped kitchen — priciest of the three.
[Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/fr/citadines-aparthotel-strasbourg.html)

### Odalys City Strasbourg Green Marsh
4★ residence near the station, ~10 min flat walk to Petite France. Kitchenette + dishwasher; parking ~€12/day (**limited — reserve at booking**); cot on request. ~€95–140/night. 8.0/10.
**Why:** good-value backup; station quarter is less charming but the walk is easy with a pram.
[Odalys](https://www.odalys-vacances.com/location-campagne/alsace-lorraine/strasbourg/green-marsh.html)

## 6. Heidelberg — Tue 7 July

> All three sit just outside the Altstadt — deliberate: Altstadt parking is scarce and the inner city is an Umweltzone (order the green Umweltplakette ~2–3 weeks ahead, ~€6–15).

### ⭐ Staycity Aparthotels Heidelberg
Aparthotel in Bahnstadt, 7 min from Hauptbahnhof; Altstadt ~10–15 min by tram. Full kitchenette incl. dishwasher; on-site garage ~€15–20 (limited — mention the car at booking); **free cot AND highchair**. ~€110–150/night. **8.6/10 Booking (6,000+ reviews)**.
**Why:** the only Heidelberg option ticking every box outright.
[staycity.com](https://www.staycity.com/heidelberg/heidelberg) · [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/de/staycity-aparthotels-heidelberg-de.html)

### Aparthotel Adagio Heidelberg
New (2024) aparthotel opposite the Hauptbahnhof. Full kitchen; underground garage ~€15–25/day; crib for small fee. On-site grocery shop — handy for baby supplies. ~€120–160/night. 8.3/10.
**Why:** newest rooms, everything in one building.
[Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/de/aparthotel-adagio-heidelberg.html)

### BoardingHouse Heidelberg
Serviced apartments by Bismarckplatz — **closest to the Altstadt: ~14 min walk**. Kitchenette; underground parking ~€15/day; cot unconfirmed (bring travel cot). ~€95–135/night. 7.9/10.
**Why:** best walking position — leave the car garaged the whole stay.
[Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/de/boardingworld-heidelberg.html)

## 7. Luxembourg City — Wed 8 July

> Luxembourg runs pricey midweek (business market) — but all public transport is **free nationwide**, so a non-central base costs nothing in practice.

### ⭐ The Central Kirchberg – Smart ApartHotel
86 apartments, Neudorf/Kirchberg; free bus/tram to the old town in 10–15 min. Full kitchen; secure basement garage €20/night; crib on request. ~€110–160/night. **8.9/10 Booking (1,020 reviews)**.
**Why:** best value-for-requirements in an expensive city — and the tight Ville Haute parking never becomes your problem.
[thecentral.lu](https://thecentral.lu/smart-aparthotel-luxembourg-kirchberg/) · [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/lu/the-central-kirchberg-smart-aparthotel.html)

### The Central City – Luxury Serviced Residence
Designer suites in the Gare district, 10–12 min flat walk to the old town. Full kitchen + **in-unit laundry** (useful mid-trip!); car-elevator parking. ~€180–220+/night — **above budget**. No cribs — bring travel cot.
**Why:** the stretch option: most walkable with secure indoor parking and a washing machine.
[Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/lu/the-central.html)

### Key Inn Appart Hotel Belair
Family-oriented residences in quiet Belair, 10–15 min walk to centre. Kitchenette; **street parking only** (overnight generally free in Belair); cot likely arrangeable — email ahead. ~€100–140/night. ~8/10.
**Why:** budget pick with family-sized units; weakest parking of the three.
[key-inn.com](https://www.key-inn.com/) · [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/lu/key-inn-appart-office.html)

## Booking priority

1. **Titisee (DaHeim)** — small property, peak season. Book first.
2. **Beaune (Sweet Home)** — only 4 apartments, 9.2 score. Book second.
3. **Luxembourg (Central Kirchberg)** — midweek business demand.
4. Strasbourg, Heidelberg, Reims, Troyes — bigger aparthotels, lower risk, but **reserve parking spaces at booking time**.

**Also order now:** Crit'Air sticker (Reims + Strasbourg) and German Umweltplakette (Heidelberg, Freiburg if visited) — both have 2–4 week lead times to the UK.

## If you book all top picks

Estimated total for the 7 nights: **~€800–1,000** + parking (~€70). Every pick has a kitchen, parking solution, and cot (or confirmed space for yours).

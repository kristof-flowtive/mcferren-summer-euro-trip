# Summer Europe Road Trip — 12-Day Outline (v2)

**Route:** Kent → Le Shuttle (Folkestone–Calais) → down through Champagne & Burgundy → Basel (3 nights) → up through the Black Forest, Alsace & Germany → Luxembourg → Maastricht → back via Belgium → Kent

**Logic of the loop:** go *down* via Burgundy and *up* via Alsace + Germany. This hits Reims, Hautvillers, Beaune, Riquewihr, Strasbourg, the Black Forest AND Heidelberg without retracing a single stretch. Daily drives run 1–3 h (your 3–4 h ceiling leaves comfortable buffer for stops and traffic).

Since you're taking your own car via Le Shuttle, the loop starts from Calais rather than Paris — driving into central Paris is a hassle with a car, and Reims is a comfortable first hop from the tunnel.

## Why these stops

- **Reims** — Champagne capital, magnificent Gothic cathedral. Perfect first night: easy motorway run from Calais.
- **Hautvillers** — the village of Dom Pérignon, vineyard views over the Marne. 35 min from Reims, ideal morning stop rather than an overnight.
- **Troyes** — keeps Day 2–3 drives short. Gorgeous half-timbered medieval old town, compact and stroller-friendly.
- **Beaune** — your Burgundy wish: the Hospices de Beaune (the historic hospital), wine capital of the Côte d'Or, surrounded by chateaux and vineyard villages.
- **Basel (3 nights)** — your anchor: two full unpacked days. With a toddler: Basel Zoo, Rhine promenade, old town. Day-trip options: Colmar (45 min), Rheinfelden thermal baths, Augusta Raurica Roman site, Vitra Campus.
- **Titisee / Black Forest** — short scenic drive from Basel. Lake, forest walks, cuckoo clocks. Touristy in season — weekday mornings help. Freiburg (50 min from Basel) is a charming alternative/lunch stop.
- **Riquewihr** — the Alsace wine-route gem, as a lunch stop (you've already done Colmar, so no overnight there).
- **Strasbourg** — "big Colmar": Petite France, the cathedral, boat tour on the Ill. Easy flat strolling with a buggy.
- **Heidelberg** — castle above the Neckar, beautiful old town and bridge. Breaks up the run to Luxembourg perfectly.
- **Luxembourg City** — dramatic UNESCO old town on the cliffs, Grund valley. Natural last stop before family.
- **Maastricht (1 night)** — relatives.
- **Ghent or Bruges** — splits the Maastricht→Calais drive in two, with a canal-side lunch.

## Practical notes (own car, UK plates)

- **Le Shuttle:** book a morning crossing Day 1; ~35 min Folkestone→Calais.
- **Swiss motorway vignette:** required (CHF 40, buy online or at border).
- **French autoroute tolls:** roughly €60–80 total (a toll tag like Emovis skips queues).
- **Crit'Air sticker (France):** needed for Strasbourg & Reims low-emission zones — order online ~2–3 weeks before travel (~€4.80).
- **German Umweltplakette:** needed for Freiburg & Heidelberg centres (~€10–15, available at TÜV stations en route).
- **UK sticker / headlamp converters** for driving on the right.
